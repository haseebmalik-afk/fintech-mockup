Paid in days, not after month-end

Withdraw on demand once verified, against a market norm of 15 days after month-end. This is the switching cost Mansour wants for creators.

One balance, many rails

Bank, gift card and PayPal all draw from the same ledger balance. Gift cards serve the Q4 pilot in Egypt, Iraq and Syria and small payouts in KSA and UAE.

Nothing is lost on failure

The balance is only debited once the bank or provider confirms. A bounced transfer puts the money straight back in the wallet.

Closed-loop, no stored value

Credits move between internal wallets. No real money leaves until a creator later taps withdraw. The ledger holds the balances; the wallets display them.

Atomic, or nothing

The brand debit and every creator credit commit together. A half-finished transfer — the failure Evgeny flagged from Tabby — can't leave one side richer than the other.

Rate card drives the amount

Each creator's price comes from their rate card in the ledger. Change the selection and the campaign total, revenue and payables all recompute.
